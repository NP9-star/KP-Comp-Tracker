Local padel market

Occupancy and estimated court revenue for four local clubs, measured from their public booking availability.

How these figures are measured

Every 30 minutes the tracker reads each club's public availability for the next 14 days and records every 30-minute court block. A block counts as occupied if it was not available at the last check before it started. Blocks that were watched going from available to taken are bookings seen happening; blocks never offered publicly may be early bookings, member-only booking windows, coaching, leagues or maintenance, so occupancy is an upper bound on paid court hire.

Revenue multiplies each occupied block by the price listed for it (or the median price for the same hour, or the price table in config.yaml for MATCHi clubs) and by the club's realisation factor. It excludes member discounts unless you lower that factor, and excludes coaching fees, events, memberships and food and drink.

Blocks with no check within three hours of their start are excluded. The singles court at Padel Maidenhead is excluded so figures compare with doubles courts.
